About Me

Hello, I'm Yixuan Fu, a PhD student in mathematics at Tsinghua University.

My research interests lie in algebraic geometry, with a particular focus on Hodge theory in complex, characteristic p, and p-adic settings.

You can reach me at fuyx23 [at] mails [dot] tsinghua [dot] edu [dot] cn.

Notes

These notes contain nothing original except for errors.

  • PD Envelopes and p-Curvature These notes were inspired by talks delivered by Arthur Ogus and present a stack-theoretic perspective on PD envelopes and p-curvature.
